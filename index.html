About TempMailCode: Why Temp Mail with Code Extraction?

Signing up for modern online services, free trials, WiFi portals, and forums frequently exposes your personal email to aggressive marketing newsletters, data broker leaks, and unwanted spam.

Unlike legacy temp mail services that force you to open marketing-heavy HTML emails, dodge tracking pixels, and hunt for small confirmation digits, TempMailCode extracts the verification passcode into an isolated, single-click copy button.

How It Works Under the Hood

  • Catch-All Inbound SMTP: Listens for incoming email traffic on port 25 for any configured domain. Every address generated is live.
  • Regex Heuristic Extraction: Parses subject lines and text bodies to isolate one-time verification tokens with confidence grading.
  • In-Memory Lifecycle: Messages never touch persistent disk storage. They reside strictly in volatile RAM and are automatically purged after 60 minutes.
  • Receive-Only Architecture: TempMailCode accepts mail but never sends any, eliminating outbound spam abuse.
Security Note: A disposable mailbox is unlisted, not private. Never use temporary email for banking, government services, or accounts you need to recover later. Review our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this temporary email address completely free?

Yes. TempMailCode is 100% free with no signup, no password, and no credit card required.

How long does a disposable inbox stay active?

Messages are kept in server RAM for 60 minutes after arrival, then automatically erased irretrievably. You can also wipe an inbox immediately with the Delete button.

Can I receive verification codes from any site?

TempMailCode works smoothly across forums, free trials, public WiFi logins, and developer staging. If a site blocks a domain, click "switch domain" in the live inbox to get a fresh active address.

How do I automate email verification in CI/CD tests?

Developers can use the keyed REST API to claim an address, poll for verification codes, and release mailboxes inside Playwright, Cypress, or Selenium suites.
